The tip formula, written on the wall (in Solidity)

What the contract rewards

Reward triggers on a verified close — never a proposal, never effort. Three requires, not a values poster.
✍️

maker ≠ checker

Two signatures on-chain. require(msg.sender != maker) — the person who did the work can't certify it for money. Encoded in code, not a handbook.

📈

compounding vesting

The payout streams; re-verifying durability extends it. A fix that keeps paying earns more than a one-shot — so people chase leverage, not a trophy.

🔒

safety = hard zero

A safety failure zeroes the unreleased reward and halts — regardless of ROI. A terminal gate, never a weighted term.

Culture first, contract second — never code an incentive you haven't first run by hand.
