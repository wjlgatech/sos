The same six habits, two outcomes

Highlight reel vs. training log

🎬 Highlight reel — goes viral
1
One clean take, best angle, takes 1–47 hidden
2
The performer says “nailed it” — and grades itself
3
Studio lights; objects pre-arranged by a human
4
No idea if it works twice
5
Impressive once; clumsy the next morning
6
Moat = the video
📓 Training log — gets boringly strong
1
Every rep recorded — the ugly ones too
2
An honest coach grades it — not the performer
3
Varied conditions on purpose (diversity > reps)
4
“Don’t get hurt” overrides every other score
5
Keeps the drills that compound; drops the rest
6
Moat = the loop
In robotics, “boring” is the highest compliment there is.
