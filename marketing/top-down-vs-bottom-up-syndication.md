## X / Twitter (thread)

**Post 1:**
"Everyone innovate!" — a slide, a rocket, and zero change by Tuesday.

You can't *order* people to close loops. You design a game where hunting them is the obviously-winning move. Here's how ↓ 🧵

**Post 2:**
Two kitchens. Top-down = the chef barks; every fix funnels through one overloaded brain. Scales to exactly one genius.

Bottom-up = the open kitchen: anyone spots a backed-up station, tries a tweak one shift, and an independent "expo" verifies it. Scales to everyone.

**Post 3:**
The trick is the contract. Pay the *verified* fix — never effort, never proposals.
• maker ≠ checker (in Solidity, not a handbook)
• reward *compounding* — the payout streams, and pays more the longer it holds
• safety = a hard zero, not a weighted term

**Post 4:**
And bottom-up isn't the fluffy option — it's the one with the citations. Open X-Embodiment: robots got better from *diversity*, not one lab dictating. A company that funnels every idea through the top is a robot trained on one blue box.
